Privacy statement

Rata & Smudge is a private app for running a small business. This page explains what it stores and who can see it.

What is stored

The business's products, recipes, ingredients, prices, selling days, sales, orders, batches and lists, plus the Google email addresses of the people the owner has added. Customer details are stored only when the owner types them into an order, or when an order arrives from the business's own Shopify store.

Where it is stored

In Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), in Google's Sydney data centre. Google encrypts it at rest and in transit. A copy is also kept on each signed-in device so the app works without signal.

Customers' email addresses

When a customer asks for a receipt by email, their email address (and name, if they give it) is kept so the receipt can be sent, and as a record of the sale. They are also asked whether they would like to join the Rata & Smudge Loyalty programme. Only customers who say yes are sent news and offers, and every such email offers an easy way to leave. Customers who say no only ever receive the receipt they asked for. The date, the wording they agreed to and who asked are kept as a record of their choice.

A customer can ask at any time to leave the Loyalty programme, or for their details to be deleted.

Who can see it

Only the Google accounts listed in the business's Setup → People. Each business is separate: no other business, and no other user of the app, can read it. Helpers can see products and record sales; they cannot see reports or change prices.

Your choices

An owner can download everything as a file, or delete the business and all its data for good, from Setup → Privacy and your data. A business has a main owner, who can never be removed, and at most one co-owner. Only the main owner can delete the business. Removing a person from the business stops their access immediately.

Questions

Contact the business owner, or the app's administrator.
