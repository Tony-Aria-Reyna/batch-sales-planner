Privacy statement

Rata & Smudge is a private app for running a small business. This page explains what it stores and who can see it.

What is stored

The business's products, recipes, ingredients, prices, selling days, sales, orders, batches and lists, plus the Google email addresses of the people the owner has added. Customer details are stored only when the owner types them into an order, or when an order arrives from the business's own Shopify store.

Where it is stored

In Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), in Google's Sydney data centre. Google encrypts it at rest and in transit. A copy is also kept on each signed-in device so the app works without signal.

Who can see it

Only the Google accounts listed in the business's Setup → People. Each business is separate: no other business, and no other user of the app, can read it. Helpers can see products and record sales; they cannot see reports or change prices.

Your choices

The owner can download everything as a file, or delete the business and all its data for good, from Setup → Privacy and your data. Removing a person from the business stops their access immediately.

Questions

Contact the business owner, or the app's administrator.
